THE MVP, EXPLAINED

One agent. One recurring problem.

Parcel helps a customer-support agent answer “Where is my order?” and delivery-problem tickets from one workspace.

  1. Open a ticket.See the customer’s question alongside the matching order.
  2. Check the facts.Review tracking events and the relevant sample store policy.
  3. Take the next step.Prepare a reply, record an escalation, or resolve the ticket.
What would we test?

Can agents handle delivery tickets faster, with fewer mistakes, than when switching between separate support and tracking tools?

This example uses six fictional orders and rule-based reply templates. It has no live store, courier, email, or AI connection. All changes reset when you reload.
